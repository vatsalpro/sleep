Scheme Overview

The National Scholarship Demo Scheme supports undergraduate and postgraduate students across India with tuition waivers and an annual stipend.

Post-Matric Scholarship: Covers students with verified annual family income below 8,00,000 INR.

Merit-cum-Means Scholarship: Awarded to top 60% scorers in 12th or degree examinations.

Application Fee Structure

Registration fee is payable once per academic cycle before submitting the final application.

Applicant Category Registration Fee Processing Time
General & OBC 1,200 INR 3 to 5 business days
SC, ST & PwD 600 INR 1 to 2 business days

Required Documents

  • Recent Passport Photo (JPG or PNG, maximum size 500 KB).
  • 10th and 12th Marksheet (PDF or JPG, maximum size 2 MB).
  • Valid Income Certificate showing family income below 8,00,000 INR (PDF or Image, Max 2 MB).

Important Dates and Deadlines

  • Portal Opening Date: December 1, 2026
  • Last Date for Online Submission: January 15, 2027
  • Verification & Hall Ticket Date: February 1, 2027
