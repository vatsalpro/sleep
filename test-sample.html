National University Admissions 2026

Published on November 11, 2026 • By Student Support Services

Introduction and Overview

Welcome to the admissions portal of National University. Our institution offers premier undergraduate and postgraduate programs across computer science, engineering, and arts.

All students are encouraged to review the eligibility criteria thoroughly before submitting their candidate profiles. Classes for the upcoming academic year will commence on August 1st.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must satisfy the following minimum qualifications:

  • Completion of 12th standard or equivalent examination with mathematics and physics.
  • A minimum aggregate score of 60 percent marks or equivalent grade point.
  • Proficiency in basic computer literacy and quantitative aptitude.

Application Fee Structure

Candidates must pay the non-refundable registration fee according to their category. All payments must be processed through the online gateway.

Required Documents

Candidates must ensure scanned copies of the following documents are ready before beginning the application:

  • Valid Domicile Certificate issued by a competent state authority.
  • Income Certificate demonstrating annual family income below 8,00,000 INR.
  • High School and 12th standard marks sheet and passing certificate.
  • Government-issued photo identification proof (Aadhaar Card or Passport).

Important Dates and Deadlines

Please mark your calendars for the following critical milestones. Applications submitted after the final deadline cannot be accepted under any circumstances.

  • Portal Opening Date: December 1, 2026
  • Last Date for Online Submission: January 15, 2027
  • Hall Ticket Availability: February 1, 2027
